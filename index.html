Takumi Shinohara

Postdoctoral Researcher at KTH Royal Institute of Technology

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Department of Decision and Control Systems, School of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science (EECS), KTH Royal Institute of Technology, under the supervision of Prof. Karl Henrik Johansson and Prof. Henrik Sandberg. I received my B.E., M.E., and Ph.D. degrees from Keio University in 2016, 2018, and 2024, respectively, under the supervision of Prof. Toru Namerikawa. My research centers on theoretical analysis to develop secure and resilient control systems, with main interests in secure state estimation and vulnerability analysis in control systems.

Previously, I worked at Mitsubishi Research Institute from 2018 to 2025 as a consultant, where I mainly conducted research on cybersecurity policy in Japan. Also, I was a visiting researcher at Keio University from 2024 to 2025.
