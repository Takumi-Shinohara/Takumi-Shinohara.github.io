I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Department of Decision and Control Systems, School of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science (EECS), KTH Royal Institute of Technology, under the supervision of [Prof. Henrik Sandberg](https://people.kth.se/~hsan/index.html) and [Prof. Karl H. Johansson](https://people.kth.se/~kallej/index.html). I received the M.Sc. and Ph.D. degrees in Engineering from Keio University in 2018 and 2024, respectively, under the supervision of [Prof. Toru Namerikawa](https://www.st.keio.ac.jp/en/tprofile/sd/namerikawa_toru.html). My research interests include the security and resilience of networked control systems.

Previously, I worked at [Mitsubishi Research Institute](https://www.mri.co.jp/en/) from 2018 to 2025 as a consultant, where I mainly conducted research on cybersecurity policy in Japan. Also, I was a visiting researcher at Keio University from 2024 to 2025.
